# Glance Studio — practical workflows

These are suggested user procedures, not claims that automated tests have been performed. Each workflow has a concrete outcome to inspect.

## A readable chat story

Keep early messages short and establish who is speaking. Give longer messages more reading time. Watch the preview without pausing; if you cannot read a bubble comfortably, simplify the text or extend its duration.

## A product-support example

Use a fictional customer and a real, supported product capability. Show a question, a concise explanation and the outcome. Do not present a scripted exchange as an authentic testimonial or imply a feature that the product does not have.

## Add voice without overwhelming the chat

Generate or record a short test with the intended character voice. Align spoken text with the message appearance, lower music where speech begins, and check names. Model downloads need network access even when later generation is local.
