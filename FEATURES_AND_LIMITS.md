# Glance Studio — features and limitations

Use this table to decide whether a workflow fits your task. Product version, device support and active plan can change what is available.

| Capability | What the product documents | Boundary to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Script | Messages, participants, typing delays and delivery states. | A staged conversation is not evidence of a real exchange; label fictional examples appropriately. |
| Presentation | 3D phone or flat chat, media inserts, backgrounds and camera motion. | The site labels some material as development previews; downloadable versions may differ. |
| Voice and export | The site describes on-device voice generation after model download and vertical MP4 output. | Generation speed and codec availability depend on device support. Confirm features in the installed version. |

## Before changing a workflow

Keep the source material until you have checked the result. A supported format or visible option does not guarantee that every input will work identically. Test the smallest example that includes the property you care about: text, timing, a formula, a recurring event, metadata or a language pair.

## Pricing and availability

Consult the current app and storefront for local prices, taxes, renewals and entitlements. These guides do not make a fixed regional price or lifetime-availability promise. Product-specific free/paid distinctions are recorded above only where the public materials describe them.
